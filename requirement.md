# System requirement analysis
## Functional requirements:
1. users can login with email and password
2. users can change the password and username 
3. users can join group (candy bowl) and send invitation to other people (friends, couple etc)
4. users can create candy (date, hangout idea) and store it in group bowl
5. each candy will have created date, author, description, title, planned date, and category
6. users in same bowl can create candy category(romantic, fun, horror etc) 
7. users can see the candies on calendar 
8. after those candies expire, users can attach photos so after that users can see the photos of corresponding candies
9. **Date Roulette:** Filter date ideas by budget, weather, or duration and let the app randomly decide your next plan.
10. **Surprise Date Mode:** Lock in a date where details remain blurred for your partner until a specified countdown timer reaches zero.
11. **Dual-Approval Swipe:** Swipe through proposed date ideas or places to eat; when both partners swipe right, it's instantly scheduled.
12. **Daily Prompts:** A shared morning Q&A prompt. Answers are revealed only after both partners submit their responses.
